OFF/NA

Print, posters and web for an independent Tel Aviv fashion platform.

Fashion Platform

Type
Print, Posters & Web
Year
2026
Role
Graphic Design, Web
Context
Client

OFF/NA is an independent fashion platform in Tel Aviv. I design what it puts out around its shows, held together by the hand-drawn mark, black, and one volt yellow.

Print

The invitation to the show at Fashion Week Tel Aviv: a members card with an NFC chip that opens the club page.

Posters

An open casting call, the show poster, and a fashion drawing competition.

Web

The OFF/NA site, and the club page the card opens.
